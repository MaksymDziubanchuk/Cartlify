# Disputes at the bank

A buyer may ask their bank to take a payment back (a chargeback). The bank opens a dispute, takes the money from the balance of the shop at once, and decides weeks later on the evidence of both sides. Most shops learn of it from a mail of Stripe and then do everything by hand: find the order, stop the parcel, collect the evidence, remember to give the money of a return back once the dispute ends, cancel what is not to be sent. Cartlify does that itself. This page is for the shop owner and for a developer of the frontend; the money journal it writes into is in [Payments](payments.md).

## What the app does by itself

- **It follows every dispute from its first event to its end.** Each event of a dispute is read again from Stripe (the state, the deadline, the evidence, the money it moved), so the order the events come in never matters and an event that is late or delivered twice changes nothing. A dispute whose step did not finish when its event came (Stripe was not reached, the database was busy) is finished by the worker within minutes.
- **It holds the parcel.** A paid order that is not sent yet is held while a dispute of its payment is open (`ORDER_ON_HOLD` for the step to shipped): the shop does not send goods it may not be paid for. The hold goes by itself when the dispute ends.
- **It drafts the evidence.** On every open dispute the app puts a draft of the evidence at Stripe once, never submitted: what was bought (the items, the delivery, the total), the name and the email of the buyer, the send (the carrier, the tracking number, when it was sent and delivered), the refunds of the payment (the day, the sum, the number of the refund) and the statements of return of the order (the day, what became of the goods and of the money). No note of the admins, no key, no internal id. A person reads it in the dashboard of Stripe, adds to it and submits it there: the evidence goes once, and it is a person's decision.
- **It keeps the money straight.** What the bank took and gave back are lines of the money journal (`money.dispute_withdrawn`, `money.dispute_reinstated`, the sum of each balance transaction, without the fee of Stripe); the reports count them in the period they happened in.
- **It stops a double refund.** While a dispute of the payment is open, the money of a statement does not go through Stripe (`RETURN_REFUND_DISPUTED`) and is not recorded as sent another way, because the bank may give it to the buyer as well. The one exception is a dispute Stripe still lets the shop settle with a refund (`isChargeRefundable`).
- **It ends what the end decides.** The shop won: the money of the statements that waited goes to the buyer at once, and an order not sent may go. The shop lost on an order not sent: the app cancels the order, puts the goods back on the shelf, counts what the bank gave the buyer and sends the rest of the payment itself, with a mail that says both sums. The shop lost with a statement waiting: the money of the bank pays the statement first, and the app sends only what is left.
- **It tells the buyer only what the buyer needs.** The buyer is never mailed that a dispute opened or was won; only a cancel and money the shop sent have their mails.
- **It shows the team what to answer.** The alert of the disputes to answer lists the open disputes without submitted evidence, the nearest deadline first, with the days left, and marks the ones whose order has a statement open; the alert of the disputes lost shows the last ones.

## The states

| `status`                                         | What it is                                                            | Money                      | Hold of an order not sent            |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `warning_needs_response`, `warning_under_review` | an inquiry of the bank: it asks questions and takes no money          | none                       | yes                                  |
| `warning_closed`                                 | the inquiry ended without a dispute                                   | none                       | goes                                 |
| `needs_response`                                 | a dispute: the money is taken, the evidence is due by `evidenceDueBy` | `money.dispute_withdrawn`  | yes                                  |
| `under_review`                                   | the evidence was submitted, the bank decides                          |                            | yes                                  |
| `won`                                            | the bank decided for the shop and gave the money back                 | `money.dispute_reinstated` | goes                                 |
| `lost`                                           | the bank decided for the buyer and kept the money for them            |                            | goes (the order is cancelled, below) |
| `prevented`                                      | Stripe settled it before it began                                     |                            | goes                                 |

A payment may have several disputes (PayPal opens one for each claim, and a buyer who lost one may open another): each is a row of its own, and the order stays held until the last open one ends. A snapshot of Stripe older than the state the app keeps (an earlier step, the same step with fewer submissions, anything after an end) changes nothing of the dispute and of the hold, while its money is still written once. An appeal of a lost PayPal dispute keeps the dispute open at Stripe until the final decision.

## Case by case

**An inquiry of the bank.** The order not sent is held and the evidence is drafted, as for a dispute; no line of money is written. When the inquiry closes the hold goes.

**A dispute of an order not sent, won.** The bank gives the money back (`money.dispute_reinstated`), the hold goes, the admins send the order as usual.

**A dispute of an order not sent, lost.** The order stays held (the buyer has the money of the bank: the goods are not to go) until the app cancels it by a statement of its own (`source: system`, `reason: dispute_lost`) on all that is left of it, the statements open beside it too: the goods go back to the stock at once with a line of the stock log, the order is `cancelled`, and every statement of it whose money goes by itself is paid. What the bank gave the buyer is shared among the items and the delivery of the order by their sums (the decision of the system, [Payments](payments.md#what-money-given-back-by-hand-or-taken-by-a-bank-was-for)): the transfer of the statement takes it first as money the buyer has back, and the rest of the payment (the total less the sum of the dispute) goes back through Stripe by an ordinary transfer, in transit and then sent. The buyer is mailed "Your order is cancelled after the decision of your bank: your bank gave you back X, we sent you the rest, Y" (`orderCancelledByBank`) once the rest is sent, or at once without the line of Y when the bank gave back all of it. The items are not split: a buyer who still wants them places a new order.

**A dispute of an order with a statement waiting.** While the dispute is open the transfer of the statement waits (the card of the statement says `disputeOpen`); everything else of the statement goes on as usual (the goods, the proof, the parcels and their cost, a lower sum). Won: the statement goes on where the dispute stopped it: the app sends the money of a statement whose money goes by itself (its goods came back at once: a withdrawal or a cancel of an order not handed over) as soon as the bank gave the money back; an admin sends the rest as usual, the deduction for the way back as always. Lost on an order the shop cancelled before it was sent: the money of the bank is shared among the items and the delivery by the system; the rows it pays whole are paid at once (the buyer is mailed that the money is with them already), the transfers of the statements take it first and ask Stripe only for what is left. Lost on an order sent: when the bank took all of the payment and no other money moved between the shop and the buyer on the order before it, the app shares it among the items and the delivery by itself, the same way; otherwise what the bank took waits for root or a money admin to say what it was for (the items and the delivery it gave back, the rest a loss of the shop), and the transfers of the order wait with it (`RETURN_BANK_MONEY_PENDING`); the decision pays at once the rows it covers whole, the transfers take its shares first. The bank gave more than the goods: the rest is a loss of the shop, plain in the journal. The bank gave less: the shop sends the rest as usual. The mail that closes the statement says what the bank gave back and what the shop sent of the rest, never "we sent you" for the money of the bank.

**A dispute of an order already sent or delivered, without a statement.** Nothing changes in the order. Won: the money is the shop's. Lost: when the bank took all of the payment and no other money moved between the shop and the buyer on the order before it, the app shares it among the items and the delivery by itself (a statement made later for them takes it first); otherwise root or a money admin says what it was for: the goods (a statement made later for them takes it first, so the buyer is never paid twice) or a loss of the shop, plain in the journal; until then the transfers of the order wait.

**A dispute of a payment the order could not take** (a late payment, whose refund failed as `disputed`). Won: the app sends the refund again. Lost: the bank gave the money back, the late payment is closed; when the bank gave less, the app sends what is left. Root may not record a failed refund of the payment as sent another way while the dispute is open (`PAYMENT_REFUND_DISPUTED`), nor once the buyer has the money (`PAYMENT_REFUND_NOT_FAILED`: the bank gave it, or the app sent it again); a failure counts in the alert of the failed refunds only while the shop still holds its sum.

**A refund Stripe refused because of a dispute** (`disputed`): no mail to the buyer and no alert of the failed refunds; the dispute is in the alert of the disputes to answer instead.

## Text for the terms of the shop

The behaviour of a lost dispute on an order that was not sent yet is a rule of the shop, and the buyer should be able to read it before paying. A text the owner can put into the terms (or the part of the terms on payments and refunds), to be checked by a lawyer and translated into the languages of the shop:

> **Payment disputes.** If you dispute a payment with your bank, we will not send the order while the dispute is open. If your bank decides the dispute in your favour before the order was sent, we cancel the order: your bank returns to you the amount it decided on, and we send you any remaining part of your payment to the payment method you used. You will receive an email with both amounts. If you still want the items, please place a new order. If the order was already sent, the dispute is decided by your bank on the evidence of both sides.

## The dashboard of Stripe

What the owner sets in the dashboard of the Stripe account, in the sandbox and again in Live mode:

- **The webhook** (Developers, Webhooks: the destination of `https://<api-domain>/api/payments/stripe/webhook`) listens to these 16 events: `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `checkout.session.async_payment_failed`, `checkout.session.expired`, `refund.created`, `refund.updated`, `refund.failed`, `charge.dispute.created`, `charge.dispute.updated`, `charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn`, `charge.dispute.funds_reinstated`, `charge.dispute.closed`, `radar.early_fraud_warning.created`, `radar.early_fraud_warning.updated`, `review.opened`, `review.closed`. Do not add `charge.refunded` and `payment_intent.*` (they repeat the events above with less) or `payout.*`. Any other event is acknowledged and left alone, so one added by mistake does no harm.
- **The ways to pay** (Settings, Payments, Payment methods): cards with Apple Pay and Google Pay, PayPal with **Settle on Stripe** (its money, refunds and disputes on the one balance of Stripe, one payout), and Bizum. Bizum works on a live account only after Stripe verified the business with its tax number. No domain has to be registered: the wallets show on the page of Stripe. Other ways Stripe turns on by default (Klarna, BLIK, Bancontact...) do not show: the page allows `card`, `paypal` and `bizum` only.
- **Disputes** (Settings, Payments, Disputes): keep **Dispute resolution** off (Stripe would give the money of disputes back by itself, past root and past the hold of the order), and turn **Enable auto-submit** and **Smart Disputes** off (they submit evidence without a person; the app only drafts it). **Dispute deflection** is the owner's choice: it gives the bank the data of the order before a dispute is opened, moves no money, and may be paid.
- **The team** (Settings, Team and security): give the people of the shop roles that cannot refund. A refund made in the dashboard is money the app learns about only afterwards and that waits for root to say what it was for (the alerts of the refunds by hand); refunds of statements, of cancellations and compensations go out through the app. Which role fits is checked on the page of the roles of Stripe, since the roles and their rights change; a custom role without the right to process refunds is the closest fit where the account has custom roles.
- **Radar**: a rule of manual review is the owner's choice. With one, Stripe holds a payment for review (`review.opened`), the app holds the order until the review closes and mails the buyer that the payment is checked.

**A failed payout** of the balance of Stripe to the bank account of the shop is not followed by the app: Stripe writes to the owner itself, and the bank account is corrected only in the dashboard of Stripe.

## The routes

| Route                                   | Who         | What                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET /api/payments/disputes`            | admin, root | the disputes, the inquiries among them, the nearest deadline first (one without a date last), a page at a time (`limit`, `cursor`, `total` of the filter). Filters: `status` (states through commas), `open`, `toAnswer` (open, nothing submitted), `closedFrom`, `orderId`, `moneyUndecided` (what the bank took waits for root to say what it was for). Each row: the ids at Stripe, the order and the buyer, `late` (a payment the order could not take), the reason of the bank, the state, `evidenceDueBy`, `hasEvidence` (also the draft of the app), `submissionCount` (0 while nothing was submitted: this, not `hasEvidence`, says the dispute waits for an answer), `isChargeRefundable`, `withdrawn` (what it took less what it gave back), `returnOpen`, the times and `draftedAt`. `400 CURSOR_INVALID` |
| `GET /api/payments/disputes/:disputeId` | admin, root | one dispute with its lines of the money journal, its order (the status, the total, `holds`) and the statements of the order, each open one with what it still owes (`moneyLeft`, for the decision on the money of the bank). `404 DISPUTE_NOT_FOUND`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
