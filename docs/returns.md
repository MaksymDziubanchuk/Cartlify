# Withdrawal and returns

This page explains how the shop takes a buyer's withdrawal from a purchase and the goods an admin takes back, to a developer who builds the frontend and to the shop owner. Every statement here is true of the code; the routes, the fields and the error codes are in [API](api.md) and in Swagger (`/documentation`), the settings are in [configuration](configuration.md). What to do step by step in each case, for the buyer, the admins and root, is in [the scenarios of returns](return-scenarios.md).

## Two rules for the frontend

- **The button of withdrawal is always there, and the server says what it does.** EU law asks for a function on the site, from 19 June 2026, with which a buyer withdraws from the contract in a few steps. While the order can be withdrawn from (`withdrawalOpen: true` in the answers about the order) the button reads "Withdraw from the contract here" and takes the statement of the buyer. The page shows `withdrawalEndsAt`, the end of the period of the contract; the shop forgives a few days more (`withdrawalGraceDays`, root sets it, never told to the buyer), so the button goes by `withdrawalOpen`, not by the date. Once `withdrawalOpen` is false the server takes no statement of withdrawal; the button then reads something else (for example "Contact us about a return") and leads to the chat of the shop. The page never counts days: it shows the date the server gives and the state it gives.
- **Ask before the statement goes.** The statement of a paid order that was not sent is final at once: the goods go back to the stock, the money back to the card, and the order is cancelled when all of it was named. The page shows a confirmation with the items and units named before it sends the request; the server takes the statement in one step, as the law asks.
- **A refund is never made in the dashboard of the payment provider.** The money of a return goes back through the app (`POST /api/returns/:returnId/refund`, or automatically with a cancel of a paid order that was not sent), because only then the statement, the stock and the reports know of it. A refund made by hand in the Stripe dashboard is not a return in the app: the app does not learn of it, so the statement, the stock and the reports do not change.

## The statement and its rows

A **statement** (`returns`) is one action: a buyer withdraws from the purchase of some goods of an order, or an admin takes goods back outside the withdrawal. It keeps what the law asks of it: when it was made, by whom and from where (the address and the browser of the request), and the name and the email the answers go to, so the statement lives on after the account is deleted. A statement is never removed. Another statement of the same order can extend it (`extendsId`).

A statement has **rows**, one for each part of an item of the order: the quantity, what became of the goods, what became of the money, the sum. A row starts as the whole of what the statement names and splits when only some of its units get a result (one unit may come back late, another be refused).

| What                                | Values                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Source (`source`)                   | `account` (the buyer, signed in), `form_code` (the public form with the code from a mail), `form_manual` (the public form without the code, checked by an admin by hand), `admin` |
| Basis (`basis`)                     | `withdrawal` (within the period, the buyer's right), `other` (an admin takes goods back outside it)                                                                               |
| Reason of an admin (`reason`)       | `defect`, `wrong_item`, `damaged`, `goodwill`, `shop_cancel`, `refused` (the buyer refused the parcel), `other`                                                                   |
| Goods of a row                      | `expected`, then `to_stock` (back on the shelf), `written_off`, `lost`, `not_accepted` (with a reason: the shop does not take it back), `not_arrived`                             |
| Money of a row                      | `not_refunded`, `pending` (on its way), `refunded`, `refused`                                                                                                                     |
| Status of an order (`returnStatus`) | `none`, `open`, `open_partial`, `partial`, `full`, `declined`                                                                                                                     |
| Close code of rows (`refusedCode`)  | `buyer_changed_mind`, `not_buyer`, `goods_not_returned`, `other` (root), `stated_again` (the database only: the buyer stated the units again, see below)                          |

A statement closes by itself when every row has its result: the goods are not expected any more and the money is refunded or refused. A row that still waits keeps it open, and the alerts of the admins remind of it; root closes rows that will never get a result without a refund, with a code and a note. The personal data of a statement is cleaned only after it is closed (see below), so a statement left open keeps it.

**The code of a statement.** Each statement has a number in its order (1, 2, 3, set by the database and never changed) and a code made of it, the one the buyer and the admins see: `W` for a withdrawal, `R` for a return outside it, then the order and the number (`W42-2`). Every mail of a statement names it in the subject and the text, the card, the list and its csv file (`code`), the buyer's view of the order and the export carry it, and the search of the list (`q`) finds a statement by its code, in any case.

**A delivered order that came back.** The status of an order does not change with its statements: a delivered order stays `delivered` when all of it came back (only a paid or shipped one becomes `cancelled`). What came back is `returnStatus`, which every place that shows the status of an order gives next to it: the answers of the orders, the list and the card of the admins, the csv of the orders (`Return status`), the profile of a user for the admins, the chat bot.

## The period

- The period starts with the delivery (`deliveredAt`) and lasts the days of the contract the order kept (14 at least; 14 when none was published), counted in calendar days of the time zone of the shop, to the end of the last day. That is the date the buyer is told (`withdrawalEndsAt`, the mails of the order, the bot). The server takes a statement `withdrawalGraceDays` more (root sets it, 4 unless set: an end that falls on a holiday moves, the grace covers it); the bot then says the period of the contract ended and the shop still takes a statement.
- The moment of the delivery is the step to delivered, or the moment the admin gives with it (`deliveredAt` of `PATCH /api/orders/:orderId/status` and of each order of `POST /api/orders/status`: the carrier delivered it before the step was made). It is never of the future and never before the order was sent (`ORDER_DELIVERED_AT_INVALID`), and only goes with the step to delivered (`DELIVERED_AT_NOT_ALLOWED`). It is for the delivery by hand: the admin takes it from the tracking of the carrier, an earlier date shortens the period of the buyer, and the admin log keeps it; the moment from the services of the carriers comes with them.
- Before the delivery (a paid or shipped order) the period has not started and the buyer may always withdraw.
- A paid order that was not sent, and a pickup that is ready and not collected, still have their goods on the shelf: a statement of the buyer (from the account or the form with the code) and a cancel of the shop take the goods back to the stock and the money back at once, and the order is `cancelled` when all of it came back. A statement and the step that sends the order (or hands the pickup over) wait for each other: whichever comes first decides.
- Each item keeps the **rule of withdrawal** it had at the confirmation (`allowed`, `sealed_hygiene`, `sealed_media`, `mixed`, `perishable`, `personalised`, `periodical`); an item without the right cannot be put into a statement, one with a condition (sealed goods) is taken back only if the condition holds.
- The server checks the period when it takes a statement; the days to send the goods (14 from the statement) and to return the money are only watched by the alerts of the admins.

## Who does what

| Who         | Route                                                                                                                | What                                                                                                                |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Buyer       | `POST /api/orders/:orderId/withdrawal`                                                                               | withdraws from the whole of an order from the account (items and units can be chosen, the reason is optional)       |
| Buyer       | `POST /api/orders/:orderId/returns/:returnId/proof`                                                                  | writes the carrier and the number of the parcel they sent back                                                      |
| Anyone      | `POST /api/withdrawals/route`, `POST /api/withdrawals/items`, `POST /api/withdrawals`, `POST /api/withdrawals/proof` | the public form (see below)                                                                                         |
| Admin, root | `GET /api/returns`, `GET /api/returns/:returnId`                                                                     | the list (filters, sorts, csv) and the card                                                                         |
| Admin, root | `POST /api/returns`                                                                                                  | a statement of an admin (also outside the withdrawal, and the cancel of an order by the shop: reason `shop_cancel`) |
| Admin, root | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/receive`                                                                                | the result of the goods of the rows                                                                                 |
| Admin, root | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/proof/confirm`                                                                          | the shop confirms the proof of the parcel (the money may go before the goods arrive)                                |
| Admin, root | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/accept`                                                                                 | accepts a statement of the form without the code as the buyer's: only then it is handled                            |
| Admin, root | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/refund`                                                                                 | the money back through the provider                                                                                 |
| Root        | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/refund/outside`, `POST /api/returns/:returnId/close`                                    | money returned outside the app, rows closed without a refund                                                        |
| Root        | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/reimbursement`                                                                          | the cost of the way back paid back to the buyer outside the app                                                     |
| Root        | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/dispute`                                                                                | the end of a dispute of the payment at the bank: lost (refunded by chargeback) or won (sent again)                  |
| Admin, root | `POST /api/returns/:returnId/shipments`, `PATCH /api/returns/:returnId/shipments/:shipmentId`                        | the parcels the shop paid to carry the goods back                                                                   |

There is no route that cancels an order by itself: an order becomes `cancelled` when all of it came back with all the money.

The list of the statements (`GET /api/returns`) filters by the days of the money too, for the money that is due (the goods came back, or the proof of the parcel was confirmed): the 14 days the law gives from the statement are over (`moneyOverdue`) or end within so many days (`moneyDueWithinDays`), and sorts by their end (`sortBy=moneyDeadline`); a statement whose goods are still on their way has no deadline of the money yet. `extended` keeps the statements of a chain of extensions or the ones on their own. The card of a statement for the admins (`GET /api/returns/:returnId`) gives everything the shop knows of it: the rows, the parcels, the account of the order and its recipient, and its history from the admin log; root is hidden from an admin there, as in the journals. It also gives two hints: `deductible`, the most the next transfer may still take off for the way back (0 when it may take none), and `warnings` (`shop_paid_parcel`: the shop paid a parcel of the statement back, so a reimbursement of the way back would pay for it twice; it is allowed, the page asks first). The card of an order for the admins (`GET /api/orders/:orderId`) gives an order with statements `returnUnits`: for each item they touched the units bought, refunded, refused, waiting, back with the shop (a unit back with the shop is not to be sent) and left to send (`toShip`), the money that went back and what the order is worth after it. The steps of the status (one order and many) mark an order with statements by its `returnStatus`. The mails "shipped" and "delivered" name only the units that travel.

## The money

- A refund has three steps: the database puts the rows under a key and marks them `pending`, the provider is asked outside the transaction, then the answer settles the rows (`refunded`, or back to `not_refunded` with the reason of the failure). A double click is refused; a refund without an answer is asked again by the worker with the same key and by the webhook of the provider.
- **Stripe settings:** the webhook endpoint of the live account must receive `refund.updated` and `refund.failed`, besides the events of the payments.
- A refund the provider refuses leaves the goods back and the money `not_refunded` with a code: `card_unavailable` (the buyer is mailed that the shop will contact them), `shop_balance`, `disputed` (a dispute at the bank: the app neither sends it again nor records it as returned outside), `unknown`. The alert `failed_refunds` keeps the statement in front of the admins; root may mark the money as returned outside the app (a way, a note and the real date). A refund the bank of the card sends back after Stripe took it as done (`refund.failed` of a refund that succeeded) is the same `card_unavailable`: the rows are `not_refunded` again, the statement opens again, the buyer is told the money did not reach them.
- **The end of a dispute.** When the bank decides a dispute, root records it with `POST /api/returns/:returnId/dispute` for every disputed row of the order: `lost` (the bank gave the buyer the money: the rows are refunded with the way `chargeback`, on the day the dispute closed, with a note; no mail, the bank told the buyer) or `won` (the bank gave the money back to the shop: the mark goes, and the money is sent again the usual way). The way `chargeback` belongs to the end of a dispute only: root never gives it by hand (`RETURN_OUTSIDE_METHOD_INVALID`). The same step is ready for the webhook of the provider that tells the end of a dispute; it is not wired yet, so root records it.
- **Goods paid ahead that never come.** After the proof of the parcel is confirmed the money may go before the goods arrive. When the parcel then never comes, the goods are `not_arrived` and the money stays `refunded`: the shop gets it back outside the app (from the carrier, or from the buyer). Money still on its way cannot be set so (`RETURN_ITEM_MONEY_SETTLED`).
- The delivery goes back with the last row that makes the order whole (a row whose transfer failed counts, it is still owed; a disputed one does not); once a unit will not come back (not accepted, not arrived, disputed), a row that has not sent its money gives its share of the delivery up. The carrier for the parcel back is paid by the buyer or by the shop as the contract the order kept says (`returnShippingPaidBy`; an order that kept no contract told the buyer nothing, so the shop pays). Root lowers a sum with a note where the buyer owes it (the goods lost value; above 0: a row of nothing could never be settled), and each lowered row leaves a line in the admin log (`return.amount_lower`, the sums before and after, the note).
- **The cost of the way back.** When the contract says the buyer pays and the shop paid the carrier (a parcel back with its cost on the statement), an admin or root takes it off a transfer of a withdrawal (`returnShippingDeduction` of the refund): at most what the parcels of the statement cost less what other transfers took, and the transfer stays above 0. When the shop pays and the buyer paid the carrier, root records the money paid back to the buyer outside the app (`POST /api/returns/:returnId/reimbursement`: the sum, the way, a note, the day; once a statement): a payment of the card is never more than the order was paid. The buyer is mailed both; the reports count the deduction as an income of the delivery (by the day the money went back) and the money paid back as spent (by the day it went).
- **The free delivery is not counted again.** A buyer who withdraws from part of a paid order before it is sent keeps the free delivery the whole order earned, even when the rest is below the threshold: the shop pays that delivery, the transfer takes nothing for it.
- **Goods that came back damaged or used.** The admin sets the units `not_accepted` with a reason (`unsealed`, `mixed`, `used`, `damaged`, `other`): their money is refused and the stock does not move. What happens to the goods then is outside the app: the shop sends them back to the buyer or keeps them, as the shop and the buyer agree. A loss of value the buyer owes (the goods were used more than needed to check them) is not a refusal: root lowers the sum of the row with a note, and the buyer is told the sum kept, never the note.
- The reports count an event in the period it happened in, and a past period never changes (see [features](features.md)).

## The public form and the code

The buyer of a deleted account, or one who did not sign in, withdraws by the public form of the frontend (`WEB_PAGE_WITHDRAWAL`). The mails of the order carry a link with a code after `#`: the code is the HMAC of the number of the order (`WITHDRAWAL_CODE_SECRET`), stored nowhere. A statement with the code is taken like one from the account; one without it (or with a wrong one) is kept for an admin to check by hand, and a paid order is not cancelled by it. Every answer to a wrong request is the same, and the form is limited by address, and by order from every address for the requests without the code of the order (a request with the code is the buyer's: nobody can use up their requests).

**The units of the form.** The page of the form shows the units the visitor may withdraw from (`POST /api/withdrawals/items`) once they showed the order is theirs, and the form checks the same facts again when it is sent. The number of the order is always needed. With the code: the name of the recipient (also for an account that is alive, without a login). Without the code: the name, the phone of the recipient and, for a delivery to an address, its country and its postal code when the order has one; a pickup has the name and the phone only. The answer has only the units with the right that no statement holds, while the period is open, without prices; every failure is the same answer and the server does the same work whatever was wrong. Besides the limits by address and by order, the requests without the code count by the facts of the recipient (`LIMIT_WITHDRAWAL_FORM_PER_DATA`: the phone, or the name), from every address. A person without the number of the order withdraws by a letter or in the chat; the contract or the privacy policy should say the form needs the number and the other ways stay open.

**The check of an admin.** A statement without the code waits for an admin, who checks it outside the app and accepts it (`POST /api/returns/:returnId/accept`, once; the card shows `acceptedAt` and `acceptedBy`) or root closes it as `not_buyer`. Until it is accepted, its goods, its proof, its money, a parcel and the way back are refused (`409 RETURN_NOT_ACCEPTED`), and it is in the alert `returns_manual_check` (the list filter `accepted=false`). One statement without the code waits per order (the database holds it): another gets `409 WITHDRAWAL_UNDER_REVIEW`, told only once the facts matched; once the first is accepted, another may come for the units left.

**A statement without the code gives way to the buyer.** Anybody who knows the number of an order and the name of its recipient can send the form without the code, so its units are held only until the buyer states them: a statement from the account or with the code takes the units it needs from an open statement without the code (oldest rows first). Only a statement no admin accepted yet gives its units up: an accepted one is the buyer's. Those units leave it: `not_arrived`, the money refused, the code `stated_again`. The rest of it stays open for the admin, and a statement left with nothing is closed by its results. The person of the statement without the code is mailed that its goods are now handled in another statement of the order, without who made it and without the units; the line of the new statement in the admin log names the statements it took them from (`takenFrom`). The buyer's view of the order counts those units as free to withdraw from. A statement of an admin takes nothing over.

## Mails

The buyer is mailed when a statement is made (with the date, the address to send the goods to, the last day to send them, who pays the carrier), when the shop made one, when the money went back, when the transfer failed (`card_unavailable`), when root paid back the way back, and when the statement is closed; the person of a statement without the code is mailed when the buyer took its units over. Each mail names the code of the statement, and gives its dates in the time zone of the shop, which it names (`SHOP_TIME_ZONE`). When root lowered a sum, the mail of the money and the mail of the end say how much was kept for the value the goods lost and for which goods, never the note of root. The mails of the order (paid, shipped, delivered) carry the days of the contract or the date until which the buyer may withdraw (the end of the contract, without the grace) and the link of the form. A mail about a statement goes to the email of the statement, also after the account was deleted; a mail of a statement of the form is greeted by the name of the form, and one of a statement without the code names no goods. Every mail of the shop ends with the same line: write in the chat of the site or to `emailReplyTo` (root sets it).

## What stays where, and for how long

A statement and its rows are never removed: the reports, the stock log and the batches stand on them. What is **personal** is emptied after the term the privacy policy gives. Root publishes the text of the policy together with the terms in days (`POST /api/root/legal-documents`, kind `privacy`), a version is never changed, and the page shows the numbers from the fields of the current one. A piece of data is kept for the **shorter** of the term of the version in force when it was collected and of the version in force now, and is cleaned a day before the term ends. Without a published policy nothing is cleaned. The cleanups are functions of the database that a periodic task runs once a day as the system.

| Data                                                                                                                                                                    | Term of the policy             | Counted from                                          | What is left                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Address and browser of a login                                                                                                                                          | `retentionLoginAddressesDays`  | the login                                             | the line: who, when, with which role                                    |
| Favorites and chats of a guest                                                                                                                                          | `retentionGuestDataDays`       | the last visit of the guest (a favorite or a message) | nothing                                                                 |
| Chats of a deleted account                                                                                                                                              | `retentionDeletedChatsDays`    | the deletion of the account                           | nothing                                                                 |
| Address and browser of a closed statement                                                                                                                               | `retentionReturnAddressesDays` | the closing of the statement                          | the statement                                                           |
| Personal data of a closed statement: the name and the email (made up), the words of the buyer, the link and the number of the parcel, the notes of the shop on the rows | `retentionReturnPersonalDays`  | the closing of the statement                          | the money, the goods, the dates and the reasons (the reports need them) |
| The recipient of an order of a deleted account: the name, the phone, the address, the note                                                                              | `retentionRecipientDays`       | the deletion of the account                           | the country, the method and the price of the delivery                   |

An order that is paid or shipped, and an order with an open statement, keeps its recipient until it is settled; so does a delivered order while its period of withdrawal may run (the days of its contract, the longest grace of 30 days and a day for the time zone), because the public form asks for the name of the recipient. An open statement keeps its data while it is open. The journals keep no words of the buyer and no number or link of a parcel (those stay on the statement only); the address and the browser of the requests of the buyer or of the form that the journal of the admins and the stock log keep for a statement are emptied with those of the statement, the lines of the admins keep theirs. The account is deleted at once (`DELETE /api/users/me`), also with an open order or an open statement, and the right of withdrawal stays: the statement goes on without the account, with mails to its email and the money to the card of the order.

**Access and portability.** A signed-in person downloads their data with `GET /api/users/me/export`, which gives every order its statements: their code, their words, their data, the parcel they sent, the rows with the money. It does not give what became of the goods (only why the money of a row was refused), what the shop paid to carry them back, the ids of the provider, the notes of the admins, and the address and the browser of a statement an admin made (those are the admin's). Of a statement of the form without the code it gives what its sender wrote (the contact, the address, the browser, the words) only when the shop took it for the buyer's (money went back for it) or its email is the one of the account, never when root closed it as not of the buyer. For a deleted account, or a request by mail, root uses `GET /api/users/:userId/export` (the same set; the person is checked outside the app: the email of the statement, the phone of the recipient, the number of an order) and the request leaves a line in the admin log. What the cleanups have emptied is not given.

## The chat bot

The bot knows each order of a signed-in customer with its statements: whether the customer may still withdraw, until when (the end of the contract; in the days of grace it says the period ended and the shop still takes a statement), for which units, and where the money is. It never files or promises a return, and points to the button of withdrawal. An order whose transfer of money failed or was disputed is left to an admin. When the bot cannot answer a user with a confirmed email (the model gave no answer, or a limit of the shop or of the customer is used up), `GET /api/chat/thread/current` has `adminCallAvailable: true` and the socket sends `thread:admin-call`: the page shows the button "call an admin" (`POST /api/chat/threads/:threadId/call-admin`). The button comes back every time the bot is down and goes away when it answers again.

## Settings

`withdrawalGraceDays` and `emailReplyTo` (root sets both, the data of the shop), `WITHDRAWAL_CODE_SECRET` (at least 32 characters, required in development and production), `WEB_PAGE_WITHDRAWAL`, `LIMIT_WITHDRAWAL_PER_USER`, `LIMIT_WITHDRAWAL_FORM_PER_ADDRESS`, `LIMIT_WITHDRAWAL_FORM_PER_ORDER`, `LIMIT_WITHDRAWAL_FORM_PER_DATA`, `LIMIT_CHAT_CALL_ADMIN_PER_USER`. Every one is in [configuration](configuration.md).
