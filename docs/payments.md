# Payments: the money journal, refunds, disputes and holds

The payment page and the return page of the buyer are described in [Features](features.md#payments). This page is about what happens to the money after it is taken: every movement of it is a line of one journal, every refund goes one way, a payment the order cannot take goes back by itself, a refund made by hand in the dashboard of Stripe is followed, a dispute at the bank is followed to its end ([Disputes](disputes.md)), and an order something holds is not sent. It is written for a developer who builds the frontend and for the shop owner; the bodies, the fields and every code are in Swagger (`/documentation`).

## The ways to pay

The frontend has one button "Pay". It opens the page of Stripe, and the buyer picks the way to pay there (`allowed_payment_method_types: card, paypal, bizum`). Stripe shows a way only where it can take the payment:

| Way                                 | Where Stripe shows it                                                                                       | What it can do                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Card, with Google Pay and Apple Pay | every buyer; a wallet on a device and a browser that has one (no domain to register for the page of Stripe) | refunds that end at once or in a few days; disputes with their evidence; an early warning of fraud of the network of the card and a review of Stripe (Radar)                                                                                   |
| PayPal                              | the countries where Stripe offers it; its money is settled on the balance of Stripe (Settle on Stripe)      | refunds up to 180 days after the payment; several disputes on one payment (each claim of the buyer is a dispute of its own, one lost may be followed by another), 2 to 19 days for the evidence, an appeal keeps the dispute open; no Radar    |
| Bizum                               | buyers in Spain, in EUR, from 0.50 to 5000                                                                  | refunds up to 395 days after the payment, ended within a few minutes by `refund.updated` or `refund.failed`; a dispute gives 40 days for the evidence; no Radar; on a live account only after Stripe verified the business with its tax number |

Each way is switched on in the dashboard of the Stripe account ([Disputes](disputes.md#the-dashboard-of-stripe)). A refund Stripe refuses because the payment is too old is a failed refund like any other: root sends the money another way.

## Who handles the money

Admins and root give the money back through the app (a transfer of a statement) and follow the refunds and the disputes. What root alone could do with the money is done by root and by a **money admin**: an admin root allowed to handle the money (`canManageMoney`, given and taken by root with `PATCH /api/root/admins/:adminId`, the way root gives the right to run the newsletters). A money admin records money sent outside the app, closes the rows of a statement without money, lowers what a row refunds, says what a refund made by hand in the dashboard of Stripe was, decides on the holds root lets go, and gets the alerts of those decisions. The right is read from the database at every request (`403 MONEY_MANAGER_REQUIRED` without it), goes away when the person stops being an admin and does not come back with the role; the profile (`GET /api/users/me`, the answer of the login) tells it in `canManageMoney`, and every change of it is a line of the admin log (`payment.manager_granted`, `payment.manager_revoked`). The functions of the database check the same right again.

## The money journal

Every movement of money is one line of `money_log`, only added to, as the stock log is for the stock. A line is written by a function of the database in the same transaction that changes the state next to it (the status of the order, the money of a row of a statement), never by the server. The state is read where it is read often (an order is `paid`, a row is `refunded`, a statement has `refundedTotal`); the movement itself is only in the journal, and the reports, the alerts, the card of a statement, the lists of refunds, the chat bot and the export of a person read it there.

A line has the sum signed (above 0 comes to the shop, below 0 goes from it, never 0 but for `money.share_moved`, which moves no money) and the currency (the one Stripe charged the payment in: a line of the money of a payment that names none takes it from the payment), what it is about (the order, the buyer, the statement, the parcel back or the send of the order, the dispute), the ids of Stripe (the payment, the refund, our key of the request, the balance transaction of a dispute, the number the bank gives to follow a refund), who did it (empty for the work of the system: the webhook, the worker), the address and the browser of the request, and the time of the database: a line counts in the period of the reports it was written in, so a past period never changes.

| Event                      | Sign | Written when                                                                                                                                          |
| -------------------------- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `money.payment`            | +    | the order is paid: two lines of one payment, the goods (`total` less the price of the delivery) and the delivery (when it costs more than 0)          |
| `money.late_payment`       | +    | a payment the order cannot take came (below): once for a payment                                                                                      |
| `money.refund`             | −    | a refund is asked of Stripe (of a statement, of a late payment) or Stripe tells of one made by hand in its dashboard; it has a state                  |
| `money.refund_failed`      | +    | a refund failed, also after it was sent: a line for each line of the refund, with the opposite sum, naming it (`coversId`) and the reason             |
| `money.outside`            | −    | root records money sent to the buyer another way (a bank transfer, cash, another way), with the way, a note and the day it really went (`happenedAt`) |
| `money.return_parcel_cost` | −    | the cost of a parcel back the shop paid for a statement                                                                                               |
| `money.shipping_cost`      | −    | the cost of a send of an order the shop owes the carrier                                                                                              |
| `money.dispute_withdrawn`  | −    | the bank took the money of a dispute (a line for each balance transaction of Stripe, without its fee)                                                 |
| `money.dispute_reinstated` | +    | the bank gave the money of a dispute back                                                                                                             |
| `money.correction`         | ±    | a person changed a sum they wrote before (the cost of a parcel back or of a send): the difference, naming the first line                              |

**The purpose** says whose money it is: `order` (the payment of an order), `return` (the money of a statement of return), `late_payment`, `hand` (a refund made by hand in the dashboard of Stripe), `dispute`, `shipping` (what the shop pays the carriers, and what a carrier paid the shop for a send), `compensation` (money root gave by the button of the app). **The part** says which part of a movement the line is: a payment and a transfer of a statement are written in parts that share one id of Stripe.

| Part        | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `goods`     | the goods of a payment or of a transfer; the goods of a transfer keep in `meta` which rows of the statement they pay (`[{ returnItemId, variantId, amount }]`), so the reports share the money by product and a past period does not change when a row changes later                                                                                                   |
| `delivery`  | the price of the delivery: of the payment, and of the transfer that makes the order whole again                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `deduction` | + of a transfer: what the shop keeps for the way back the buyer owes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `covered`   | + of a transfer: the part that money the buyer already has from another source covers (a refund made by hand, the money of a dispute the shop lost, by what the decision gave the items and the delivery of the order), one line for each source and item (`coversId` the source, `meta` `[{ returnItemId, orderItemId, amount }]`); Stripe is asked for the rest only |
| `whole`     | a movement without parts                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

Example: a transfer of a statement for goods of 100 and a delivery of 10, with 8 kept for the way back, is three lines under one key: −100 `goods`, −10 `delivery`, +8 `deduction`; Stripe is asked for 102.

**The state of a refund.** A line `money.refund` is `in_transit` from the moment it is written (before Stripe is asked) and becomes `sent` once, when Stripe tells the money is sent (with the time, `stateAt`). Nothing else of a line changes: the sum, the time and the key never do. A failure is never a change of the state: it is a new line `money.refund_failed`, so a refund that failed keeps the state it had and the failure next to it. The few things a line may still take are written once, from empty, and the guard of the table (`money_log_append_only`) refuses any other change: the id of the refund at Stripe, the state `sent`, the number of the bank, what money given back by hand or taken by a bank was for (`moneyUse` with its shares in `meta`, written together, and again while no line took the shares), and the cleaning of the notes, the words of Stripe, the address and the browser when their term of keeping is over ([Returns](returns.md#what-stays-where-and-for-how-long)).

**Why a refund failed**, in our words (`failure`) and in the words of Stripe (`failureText`, for the shop; the buyer never sees them):

| `failure`          | Stripe says                                                                                                                                        | What to do                                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `card_unavailable` | `lost_or_stolen_card`, `expired_or_canceled_card`                                                                                                  | the card can no longer take money: send it another way                                                             |
| `shop_balance`     | `insufficient_funds`                                                                                                                               | the balance of the shop at Stripe is short: top it up and try again                                                |
| `disputed`         | `charge_for_pending_refund_disputed`, a refusal `charge_disputed`                                                                                  | the buyer disputed the payment at the bank: the bank settles it, the dispute is followed ([Disputes](disputes.md)) |
| `already_refunded` | a refusal `charge_already_refunded`, a refusal of a sum larger than what is left (`amount_too_large`, or a refusal of the `amount` without a code) | the money went back already, by hand in the dashboard: the lines of those refunds count it                         |
| `unknown`          | anything else, and `canceled`                                                                                                                      | try once more, then send it another way                                                                            |

**The returned total of a statement.** `returns.refundedTotal` is how much the buyer got back by the statement in all. The database counts it with a trigger from the lines of the statement (all but what the shop paid the carriers): the transfers (also on their way) less what failed, the money sent outside the app, the way back paid back; the parts covered count too, unless the money they took never reached the buyer (a refund made by hand that failed and that root has not sent another way: the rows stay refunded, the statement owes that sum again until root sends it). In the example above it is 102.

**Who reads it.** Admin and root read the journal at `GET /api/admin/money-log` (below), the card of an order of an admin has its lines in short (`money`), the card of a statement its own (`money`), the card of a dispute its own. A buyer reads none of it directly: the order of the buyer shows the money of its statements (`refundedTotal` and `refunds`, a movement at a time), the export of the person lists the money of their orders without the ids of Stripe, the keys, the notes and the actors.

## One way for every refund

Every refund the app asks of Stripe goes the same three steps, whatever it is for (a statement, a late payment, the rest after a dispute, a compensation):

1. **The lines go first.** The function of the database writes the lines `money.refund`, in transit, under a new key of the request (`transferKey`), before Stripe is asked. A double click finds the rows already on their way and is refused; the same key asked again answers the lines that are there.
2. **Stripe is asked outside the transaction**, with the key as the key of idempotency and in the metadata of the refund (`metadata.transfer`).
3. **The answer settles the lines** (`settle_money_refund`, the one end of every refund of the app): `pending` writes the id of the refund; `succeeded` makes the lines `sent` (the buyer is mailed); `failed` writes a line `money.refund_failed` for each part, and the rows of a statement become `failed` (the statement opens again when it was closed). Each answer is taken once, in any order: `sent` after a failure changes nothing, a failure after `sent` (the bank sent the money back) writes the failure lines.

Stripe that does not answer, or keeps a refund pending, leaves the lines in transit. The webhook ends them by the events of the refund, and the worker asks for the ones that stay (below). Nothing turns silence into a failure: a refund in transit for days shows in an alert.

## A payment the order cannot take

The webhook (and the timer of the reservation, below) records every payment of a page with the database function `pay_order`. When the order cannot take the money, the answer is the reason of a refund:

| Reason                       | What happened                                                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `OUT_OF_STOCK`               | the reservation of the order was over and some items were no longer in stock              |
| `ALREADY_PAID_OTHER_PAYMENT` | another payment paid the order already (the buyer paid two pages); the order stays paid   |
| `AMOUNT_MISMATCH`            | the order changed after the page was opened, the sum is not the sum paid                  |
| `ORDER_NOT_PAYABLE`          | the order could not take a payment any more (for example it was cancelled)                |
| `ORDER_NOT_FOUND`            | the order was deleted by its owner (the line keeps the number of the order in its `meta`) |

The payment is a line `money.late_payment` (+, once for a payment) and the refund of all of it a line `money.refund` in transit, both in one transaction, under the key `refund:<payment id>`. Then Stripe is asked, and its answer settles the line as above. The same payment delivered again does not sell the order: the webhook answers `200` with `skipped: REFUNDED`, and the page the buyer returns to tells it (`order.paymentRefunded` of `GET /api/payments/stripe/checkout/sessions/:sessionId`, read from the journal).

- **The buyer is mailed** when the money is sent ("We sent your payment back", once, with the move to `sent`), when the refund fails (that the shop will write about another way; also after it was sent), and when root records the money sent another way. A refund that failed because the money went back already or because of a dispute is not mailed.
- **A refund made by hand at the same time.** When Stripe refuses our request because the payment was refunded already, the app first writes every refund made by hand of the payment that the journal does not have yet; then our line either fails as `already_refunded` (the refunds made by hand gave all the money back and have their own lines, so the movement is counted once) or takes the id of the refund made by hand (`byDashboard`). What is left of the payment goes back under a new key, `refund:<payment id>:<cents left>`.
- **After a dispute.** A refund of a late payment that failed as `disputed` is sent again when the shop wins the dispute; when the shop loses it, the money of the bank counts and only what is left goes back (`money_paid_left`: the sum of every line of the payment).

## A refund made by hand in the dashboard of Stripe

The shop should give money back through the app. When somebody gives it back by hand in the dashboard of Stripe, the app is told by the event `refund.created` (a refund without our key) and follows it: one refund of Stripe is one line `money.refund` with the purpose `hand`, in transit or sent as Stripe says (`record_hand_refund`; a later event of the same refund finds the line and settles it as the events of our refunds do). The reason picked in the dashboard is only a hint for root; "fraudulent" changes nothing. What the refund means depends on the payment it gave money of:

| Situation                                                                                     | What the app does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A: the payment is one the order could not take                                                | the line of our refund in transit of the same sum takes the refund (its id, `byDashboard`); otherwise it is a line of its own and the app sends what is left of the payment itself                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| B: a paid order not sent, no statement open, the refunds by hand gave all of its payment back | the system cancels the order by a statement of its own (`source: system`, `reason: stripe_refund`) on all that is left: the goods go back to the stock at once, the refunds by hand are shared among the items and the delivery by their sums and pay the rows of the statement at once as money the buyer has back, Stripe is asked for nothing, the buyer is mailed "the order is cancelled and the money sent" (`orderCancelledRefunded`) |
| C: a paid order not sent, no statement open, part of the payment back                         | the order is held (`hand_refund`) and root decides: `POST /api/orders/:orderId/holds/hand_refund/decision` with `ship` (the refund is a compensation, the order may go) or `cancel` (a statement of the shop, `shop_cancel`, on all that is left; the refunds by hand are shared among the items and the delivery, its transfer takes them first and asks Stripe for the rest; the usual mails of a cancel by the shop)                      |
| D: a statement of the order is open                                                           | nothing is decided by itself: the transfers of the order wait (`RETURN_HAND_REFUND_PENDING`) until root says what the refund was for ([below](#what-money-given-back-by-hand-or-taken-by-a-bank-was-for)): the units it gave back pay the rows of the statement first, the rest is a compensation and the statement goes on as always                                                                                                        |
| E: the order was sent, delivered or cancelled and no statement is open                        | an expense the app cannot explain: root says what it was for (a compensation, or the goods a later statement may take it for), the alert stays until then                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

A refund made by hand that fails later is a line `money.refund_failed` like any other. A: as a failed refund of a late payment. B, and C after `cancel`: the order stays cancelled and the goods on the shelf, the buyer is mailed that the shop could not send the money and will write, root sends it another way (`POST /api/payments/refunds/:refundId/outside`). C after `ship`, E as a compensation: the alert, root decides. A refund whose shares paid rows of a statement (B, D, a later statement): the rows stay refunded and the `refundedTotal` of the statement falls by the sum that failed, so the debt is exactly that sum; the buyer, told the money is back, is mailed that it did not reach them; root sends it another way (`/outside`, the line names the refund) and the total comes back. One case is for root to know: root decided `ship` (a compensation) and the buyer later withdraws from the whole order; the transfer of the statement then meets `RETURN_REFUND_EXCEEDS_PAID` (Stripe never gives back more than the payment), and root sends the difference another way.

## A compensation by the button of the app

Root or a money admin gives the buyer of an order money through its payment at Stripe: a gesture, money for a part the buyer kept (`POST /api/payments/compensations` with `{ orderId, amount, note }`). It is a refund of the app like any other (the steps above): one line `money.refund` of the purpose `compensation`, at most what is left of the payment (`COMPENSATION_EXCEEDS_PAID`: Stripe gives back no more than it took; more goes another way), never while a dispute of the payment is open at the bank (`COMPENSATION_DISPUTED`), and not while another compensation of the order is on its way (`COMPENSATION_IN_TRANSIT`: a second press of the button, or two people at once, would give the money twice). Stripe sends most refunds at once, but it may keep one pending for days for some ways of paying: the next compensation of that order waits as long, and the alert `compensation_refunds_in_transit` shows the pending one after its days. The buyer is mailed "We sent you a compensation for order #42" once Stripe sent it. A failure is not mailed: the refund is among the failed refunds (`GET /api/payments/refunds?purpose=compensation&state=failed`) and root sends it another way (`POST /api/payments/refunds/:refundId/outside`), the buyer is told how. The note goes into the admin log (`payment.compensation`), never to the buyer.

## Money the shop got back outside the app

Root or a money admin records money that came to the shop outside the app, on the day it really came (a line `money.received`, above 0, with the way, the note and the day; no mail):

- **From the buyer, for rows of a statement** whose money the buyer had: goods paid ahead that never came, more than the statement owed (`POST /api/returns/:returnId/money-received` with `{ items: [{ itemId, amount }], method, note, at }`). Each row at most what it cost less what came back for it before; the line keeps the rows in `meta` for the reports by product, the statement has that much less back (`refundedTotal`), its rows stay as they are.
- **From the buyer, of what is overpaid**: money already with the buyer gave the goods of the order more than its statements took (`POST /api/returns/:returnId/overpaid/received` with `{ amount, method, note, at }`; [Returns](returns.md#money-already-with-the-buyer-for-goods-not-taken)). A line for each share it pays back, of the purpose of the line of the share (`hand`, `dispute`), naming it (`coversId`), the item or the delivery in `meta`; no statement. Given up instead (`POST /api/returns/:returnId/overpaid/lost`), each share is a line `money.share_moved` of 0, the only line that moves no money: what part of the line was for changes (`moneyUse` `compensation` or `loss`, the sum in `meta`).
- **From a carrier, for a send of the order** it lost or damaged (`POST /api/orders/:orderId/shipments/:shipmentId/carrier-money` with `{ amount, method, note, at }`): a line of the purpose `shipping` that names the send, money of the shop. It is no cost of the send: the cost of a send is its lines `money.shipping_cost` with their corrections.

## What money given back by hand or taken by a bank was for

A refund made by hand in the dashboard of Stripe and what a bank took for a dispute the shop lost are money of the order, not of a statement. Each line waits for a decision of what it was for, written into the line (`moneyUse` and `meta`; made again while no statement took its shares, the admin log keeps the old one): the items of the order with the units and the money, with a copy of the price (`[{ orderItemId, productId, variantId, quantity, unitPrice, amount }]`), and the delivery (`{ delivery }`). An item never gets more than its units cost, with the decisions of the order together; the delivery never more than the order paid for it; the shares never more than the line. All of the line given so, it was for the goods (`goods`); the rest is a compensation the shop gave (`compensation`, by hand) or a loss of the shop (`loss`, the bank).

- **Who decides.** Root or a money admin: `POST /api/payments/money-lines/:moneyLogId/decision` with `{ items, delivery?, note }`. The system decides by the shop's rule, sharing the line among the items and the delivery by their sums, less what the statements of the order already paid of each with money of their own (the cents of the rounding go to the delivery, or with no delivery to the dearest item): the refunds by hand that gave all of the payment of an order not sent back (B), the refunds by hand of an order root cancels (C, `cancel`), what the bank took for a lost dispute of an order not sent, or of one the shop cancelled before it was sent while its statement waits for money ([Disputes](disputes.md)), and, whatever the order is, a line that is all of the payment with no other money between the shop and the buyer on the order before it (a refund, a compensation, money outside the app or from the buyer, a dispute; the costs of the parcels and what a carrier paid do not count): it pays the rows of the open statements it covers at once, their buyer is mailed. Root's `ship` (C) makes the refund a compensation.
- **What waits.** While such a line has no decision the transfers of the order and the money sent another way wait: `RETURN_HAND_REFUND_PENDING` (a refund made by hand), `RETURN_BANK_MONEY_PENDING` (the bank, a dispute lost on an order sent).
- **What it pays.** The shares are money the buyer already has: every transfer and every step outside the app of a statement of the order takes them first for the items of its rows and the delivery that goes with them (the part `covered`, naming the line), and asks Stripe only for the rest. At the decision the rows of the open statements that the shares pay whole (with the delivery due) are paid at once, nothing asked of Stripe, and the buyer is mailed that the money is back: "The money for order #42 is already with you", with what the bank gave or what was sent earlier, or the mail that closes the statement.
- **When it fails.** A refund made by hand that fails gives no share to a later statement until root sends it another way; a statement that took it owes the sum again ([above](#a-refund-made-by-hand-in-the-dashboard-of-stripe)).

## Disputes, in short

A dispute (or an inquiry of the bank that takes no money) is read from Stripe at each of its events and followed by the app: the money it moves is in the journal, an order not sent is held, a draft of the evidence is put on it once and never submitted, and when it ends the app sends the money of the statements that waited, or cancels an order not sent the bank decided for the buyer and sends the rest. The whole logic, the text for the terms of the shop and the settings of the dashboard: [Disputes](disputes.md).

## Early warnings of fraud and reviews of Stripe

Both watch cards only.

- **An early warning of fraud** (`radar.early_fraud_warning.created`, `.updated`; only when Stripe says the shop can act on it): a paid order not sent is held (`fraud_warning`), one already sent is marked (`fraudWarnedAt`) for an alert of root. Root decides on a held order: `POST /api/orders/:orderId/holds/fraud_warning/decision` with `ship` or `cancel` (a statement of the shop on all that is left, the money back through Stripe, the usual mails). A dispute that comes later stands next to it in the list of the holds.
- **A review of Stripe** (`review.opened`, `review.closed`): Stripe holds a payment for the shop to look at, only when the owner turned on such a rule of Radar. A paid order not sent is held (`payment_review`) until the review closes; the app lets it go. Money given back from the review is an ordinary refund made by hand.
- **The buyer** is mailed once, when the order is first held for a check: "We received your payment and check it once more before we send the order; we will write when we send it or give the money back" (`orderPaymentCheck`), never the word fraud (a warning may be wrong). The order of the buyer shows `paymentCheck: true` while it is held so. After the decision come the usual mails (shipped, or cancelled with the money back).

## Order holds

`orders.holds` is the list of what holds a paid order from its sending, each `{ reason, since, ref }`. The order keeps its status (`paid`); while the list is not empty the database does not let it be shipped: `PATCH /api/orders/:orderId/status` answers `409 ORDER_ON_HOLD` with `details: { orderId, holds }` (the reasons, each once), and the step of many orders (`POST /api/orders/status`) answers the same for that order while the others take the step.

| Reason           | `ref`                             | Put by                                                   | Taken away by                                                                                                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `dispute`        | the id of the dispute             | an open dispute or an inquiry of the bank of the payment | the system, when the last open dispute of the payment ends                                                               |
| `hand_refund`    | the id of the line of the journal | a refund made by hand of part of the payment (C)         | root, money admin: the decision on the order or on the line of the refund; the app when the refund fails (no money went) |
| `fraud_warning`  | the id of the warning at Stripe   | an early warning of fraud                                | root, money admin: the decision on the order                                                                             |
| `payment_review` | the id of the review at Stripe    | a review of Stripe                                       | the system, when the review closes                                                                                       |

A hold is put only on a paid order; the cancel of the order (by any statement) empties the list. A dispute of an order already sent holds nothing (a shipped order cannot be held back from its delivery); when the admin takes the send back (shipped to paid), the order is held again for each open dispute of its payment and each refund made by hand root has not decided on. A dispute lost on an order not sent keeps its hold until the system cancels the order. The list of the orders of an admin filters by it (`holds`: reasons through commas, `any`, `none`) and by `fraudWarnedFrom`; every answer about an order for an admin carries `holds`, `fraudWarnedAt` and `money`.

## The webhook

`POST /api/payments/stripe/webhook` (the signature of Stripe, no CSRF token, outside the request rate limit) takes these events; any other is acknowledged and left alone:

| Events                                                                                   | What the app does                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`                 | the order is paid (one mail "paid"), or the money is a late payment and goes back                                                                                                                                                    |
| `checkout.session.async_payment_failed`, `checkout.session.expired`                      | the reservation is released (an expired page after the grace window for a last payment)                                                                                                                                              |
| `refund.created`, `refund.updated`, `refund.failed`                                      | a refund of the app (our key in `metadata.transfer`) settles its lines; one without the key is a refund made by hand; a new number of the bank is written to the lines of the refund; `requires_action` is only a warning in the log |
| `charge.dispute.created`, `.updated`, `.funds_withdrawn`, `.funds_reinstated`, `.closed` | the dispute is read from Stripe and followed                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `radar.early_fraud_warning.created`, `.updated`                                          | the order is held, or marked when it was sent                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `review.opened`, `review.closed`                                                         | the order is held until the review closes                                                                                                                                                                                            |

**The answers.** An error answer makes Stripe deliver the event again for three days, so the webhook answers an error only for what passes by itself or for a fault of the code:

- `401 STRIPE_SIGNATURE_INVALID` (and `400` for a missing signature or a body that is not JSON): the secret of the webhook is being changed;
- `409 RESOURCE_BUSY`: the database was busy or the transaction ran out of its time; nothing was changed;
- `503 PAYMENT_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`: Stripe could not be reached to read the dispute or the refund the event names;
- `500`: a fault of the code; Stripe delivers the event again while it is mended, and writes to the owner that the webhook fails.

An event that can never pass is acknowledged with `200 { received: true, skipped }` and a warning in the log, and what needs a person is in the journal, the alerts or the log:

| `skipped`                                                                                    | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `OBJECT_INVALID`                                                                             | the object of the event is not the kind its type names                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `NOT_AN_ORDER`                                                                               | a page the app did not open for an order, or the payment of a refund made by hand, of a dispute, a warning or a review that is neither the payment of an order nor a late payment (another tool in the same Stripe account): its money is left alone |
| `ORDER_NOT_FOUND`, `ORDER_NOT_CONFIRMED`, `ORDER_STATUS_NOT_WAITING`, `ORDER_ITEMS_REQUIRED` | the order of the page is gone, or not in the state the event needs                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `REFUNDED`                                                                                   | the payment is a late payment delivered again: its money goes back or went back (no warning)                                                                                                                                                         |
| `UNKNOWN_REFUND`                                                                             | a refund under a key of the app that no refund of the app has                                                                                                                                                                                        |

A refusal of Stripe to make a refund is never an error answer: it is a line of the journal. What the app asks of Stripe again is the worker's, never a repeated event.

**The money taken while the event did not come.** The timer of the reservation closes the page of Stripe when the reservation is over. When Stripe answers that the page was paid, the timer reads the paid page from Stripe and takes the very step the webhook takes (`settlePaidPage`): the order becomes paid with its lines and the mail "paid". The event that comes later answers `ALREADY_PAID_SAME_PAYMENT` and changes nothing. Stripe that gives no data of the page fails the job, which is tried again.

## The worker

The worker of the money (`src/workers/moneyRefund.worker.ts`) runs every minute in the process of the server; it needs the database only, not Redis.

- The refunds of the app in transit for more than 2 minutes (the transfers of the statements and the refunds of late payments): a refund with an id is read from Stripe; one without an id is looked for among the refunds of its payment by our key, and asked again with the same key when there is none. Asking again after a day is safe: the search goes through the refunds of the payment itself. A refund in transit for more than an hour is a warning in the log.
- The refunds made by hand that Stripe keeps pending, for more than 2 minutes: each is read from Stripe by its id and its line follows as the event would make it (the event may be lost).
- The disputes whose step did not finish when their event came: open without a draft of the evidence and with nothing submitted, lost on an order not sent that is still paid, ended in the last 30 days with a statement of the order whose money waits and could go now. Each is read from Stripe and followed as the webhook follows it.

The work of the system (the webhook, the worker) is in the money journal only.

The three kinds of held orders list the order paid first first (the buyer waits since they paid); each row names since when its hold waits (`holds[].since`).

A failure the shop has to act on leaves out what waits for someone else:

- while a dispute of the payment is open at the bank, any failure of it waits for the bank: the end of the dispute decides where the money goes;
- a refund of a late payment or made by hand that Stripe refused for a dispute (`disputed`) waits for the dispute even once it ended: the app sends the payment back again when the shop wins, the bank gave it when it lost;
- a failure of a late payment or of a refund made by hand counts only while the shop still holds its sum of the payment: after a dispute the bank gave the buyer the money, or the app sent the payment back again, and root may not send it another way (`PAYMENT_REFUND_NOT_FAILED`);
- the transfer of a statement that Stripe refused for a dispute is back among the failures once no dispute of the payment is open, its rows still owing the money; it also waits while a refund of the payment made by hand waits for root.

Root may be more than one: each root follows the kinds for themselves, and a case leaves every list as soon as one of them acted.

## A walk through a refund that fails

1. The buyer pays the page of a reservation that is over. The webhook answers `200`; the journal has `money.late_payment` (+51) and `money.refund` (−51, in transit); Stripe keeps the refund pending, so the buyer hears nothing yet.
2. Stripe tells the refund failed (`refund.failed`). The journal gets `money.refund_failed` (+51, `card_unavailable`); the buyer is mailed that the shop could not send the payment back and will write about another way.
3. An admin sees the alert `late_refunds_failed`, opens its list (`GET /api/payments/refunds?purpose=late_payment&state=failed`) and finds the refund with its `id`, the sum and the reason; the journal shows its lines (`GET /api/admin/money-log?paymentId=`).
4. The admin and the buyer agree on a way; the money is sent by hand. Root records it: `POST /api/payments/refunds/:refundId/outside` with the way, a note and the day.
5. The journal gets `money.outside` (−51), the admin log `payment.refund_outside`, the refund leaves the alert and the list, and the buyer is mailed that the money was sent.
